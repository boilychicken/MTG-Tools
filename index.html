A backup holds your collection, draft pool and price history in one file. Export one now and then, since browser storage can be cleared.

Import or update your collection

Export a CSV from Moxfield, Archidekt, Manabox or Deckbox (it needs a Name column; Count or Quantity is optional). A plain list such as 2 Sol Ring also works. Card details come from Scryfall, so you need to be online.

Collection search

Searches card names, types and rules text across everything you own. Try dinosaur, "draw a card", t:artifact, o:trample or -t:land.

Price tracker

Prices are Cardmarket averages from Scryfall, converted from euros to pounds. Foil copies use the foil price. With automatic recording set up, a snapshot is saved every night; without it, the app records one the first time you open it each day.

Automatic daily recording

A free GitHub job can record prices every night, even when you don't open the app. It records your cards plus every card currently legal in Standard, so a card you add from Standard already has history. Cards from outside Standard start when they're added to cards.json. That list holds names only, no quantities or values. See SETUP.md for the steps.

Saved decks

Decks are saved in this browser, grouped into folders you choose when saving. Open a deck to check it: cards you don't own show in red with their price. Export a backup from the home page to move your decks to another device.

Deck checker

Paste a decklist from Moxfield, Archidekt, ManaBox or plain text. You'll see which cards you own, what the missing ones cost in pounds, and a set of deck health checks. Commander decks also get an estimated Commander Bracket.
